# Changelog

## 1.0.0-beta
First public release of notbad-dispatch (the successor of notbad-livemap).

- Dispatch console in the browser: live unit map, calls, sending units, call signs
- Units in game: call card with distance, blip and route, offers, call list, arrow keys and G, status, panic
- Radio between the console and the game (pma-voice, SaltyChat, TokoVoip)
- Dispatcher accounts created on the console, generated passwords, guessing protection
- Call priorities, templates, history, several dispatchers at once
- In-game texts in English, Czech, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Polish

Tested in game on ESX with pma-voice.
